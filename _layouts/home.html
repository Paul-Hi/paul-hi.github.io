Paul Himmler

PhD Candidate in Visual Computing

I am a PhD candidate under the supervision of Prof. Dr.-Ing. Tobias Günther at the Professorship for Visual Computing at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg.

My research primarily focuses on inverse rendering and variational optimization problems in visual computing as well as grid-free Monte Carlo methods for solving partial differential equations. This involves the development of numerical optimization techniques that are highly parallelized on GPUs. I am passionate about collaborative research and thrive in interdisciplinary environments that inspire the exploration of novel and complex research paths.
